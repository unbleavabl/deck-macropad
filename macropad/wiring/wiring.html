The wiring

Lid above, plate below, to scale

Lay the case out like this while you wire: the lid inside-up at the top, the plate upside down below it, their back edges together. The plate carries the bare copper buses (thick lines: one per row, one per column, a GND and a 3V3 loop) and short insulated jumpers from every part to them. The lid holds the BlackPill, the multiplexer and the two buttons. The 27 wires between them are soldered at the plate end, gather along the plate's back edge into five flat ribbons, and end in five multi-pin Dupont plugs, A to E, on male header pins on the BlackPill and the multiplexer, so the lid unplugs. Each wire's number is on its line in the fold band and in the table below: tape it at both ends. Mark pin 1 of each plug and its header so it only goes on one way.

How each part connects

Buses, parts and the lid

Buses

Bare solid copper, laid first and soldered at every joint: one wire per row and per column across the keys, and a GND and a 3V3 pair twice: across the front, just in front of the screens, and across the slider ends, joined by two insulated wires up the slider-side edge. Bare copper never crosses a circuit board. Where two buses cross, slide a piece of sleeve or heat-shrink over the upper one: bare copper on bare copper shorts. Each bus takes one numbered wire to its BlackPill pin.

Keys

The switch pin nearer the front sits on its column bus: solder it straight on. The other pin takes the diode's plain red end; the black band end goes to the row bus.

Knobs

Push pins: the one nearer the keys takes a diode to row 0 (E2) or row 2 (E1); the other joins column 3, an insulated wire up the knob-column wall from E1 to E2. C: a short jumper to the GND pin of the knob's own screen. A and B: numbered wires up the wall to the BlackPill, no diode.

Sliders

Pins 1 and 2 are at the back end, pin 3 alone at the front end. Pin 1 to the 3V3 bus across the slider ends; pin 3 by a short jumper to the GND pin of the slider's own screen, just in front of it. Pin 2 is a numbered wire to A1, A2 or A3, with a 100 nF capacitor standing beside the slider body, on its right as you look at it from the back: one leg to pin 2 by a short jumper, the other leg to the GND bus across the slider ends. The capacitor has no polarity.

Screens

4-pin I2C modules (SDA, SCL, GND, VCC, left to right from the back), all at the same address. VCC and GND by jumpers down to the front pair; S5's run down the gap between the keys and the knob column. SDA and SCL are a numbered pair to the screen's channel on the multiplexer: S1 on SD0 and SC0, up to S5 on SD4 and SC4. S1 to S3 run up between the sliders, S4 and S5 up the wall.

Lid

BlackPill upside down in its cradle (chips down, USB-C at the back), multiplexer glued right of it seen from above, both with male header pins, long side up, so the numbered wires plug straight on. Solder L5 to the board's own BOOT0 button before the board goes in. Mux VIN to 3V3, GND to G, SDA to B7, SCL to B6; leave A0 to A2 and RST unconnected (address 0x70). Power everything from 3V3 only.

Close-up

One encoder up close

An EC11 takes the place of a key. A printed 3 mm spacer ring on its bushing makes it sit lower, so the shaft doesn't stand too tall above the plate. The nut goes on from the top, without its washer.

Pin reference

BlackPill pins and the buttons

Seen from above as it sits in the lid cradle: upside down, chips and the board's own buttons facing the lid, back side up, USB-C pointing at the back wall. The male header strips go in from the chip side, so the long pins stick up out of the back and the short ends sit on the cradle rails; the pin names are printed next to each hole: check them before plugging each wire on.

Wiring checklist

0 of 11 done
Before closing the case

Test with a meter, USB unplugged

  1. Beep setting, probes on 3V3 and G: it must not beep.
  2. Diode setting, red probe on the column pin, black on the row pin, for example A7 and A4 for K1. Press: about 0.5–0.7 V. Release: nothing. Repeat for all 14 positions: 12 keys and both knob pushes.
  3. A reading with the key released means that row and column touch somewhere. Nothing when pressed usually means a diode is in backwards.
  4. Knobs: beep setting between B1 and G; turning knob 1 clicks it on and off. Same for B8, then B9 and A0 for knob 2.
  5. Sliders: resistance setting between A1 and G; moving slider 1 runs it smoothly from 0 to about 10 kΩ. Same for A2 and A3.
  6. Screens: beep each screen's VCC to 3V3 and GND to G. Its SDA and SCL beep only with its own mux channel pads (S1 SD0 and SC0, and so on).
  7. Buses: beep each row bus against each column bus with no key pressed: none may beep. If one does, a sleeve has slipped at that crossing.
